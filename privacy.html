Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

NetJack is an offline, single-player game developed by İbrahim Kara. This policy describes the game and support communications.

Information in the game

NetJack does not send gameplay information or personal information to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated server connection. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Third parties, consent, and deletion

NetJack does not share any user data with third parties, and it does not integrate analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Because the app collects no personal data, no consent is requested and none needs to be withdrawn. All gameplay data is stored only on your device; deleting the app permanently removes it. If you contacted support by email, you can request deletion of that correspondence at any time (see “Support email” below).

Progress stored on your device

Progress, earned gold, unlocked looks, upgrades, scores, achievements, and settings are saved locally in the app’s storage. These records let the game resume your progress and remember your choices. There is no developer-operated cloud synchronization. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings. Removing the app or its data may erase these records.

Purchases

NetJack is a paid App Store download. Apple processes the download purchase under its policies; the game does not receive your payment card details. The in-game Shop uses only gold earned by playing. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Support email

If you contact adelcodelabs@gmail.com, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to answer your request. Email is handled by the email service provider. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence using the same address.

Apple services and this website

Apple may process purchase or diagnostic information through its own services and your device settings. See Apple’s privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses when you visit it; see GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please avoid including a child’s personal information in support messages.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to adelcodelabs@gmail.com. Developer: İbrahim Kara.
