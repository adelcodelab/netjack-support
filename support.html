Support

Need help with NetJack? Email adelcodelabs@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

How to play

Press the playfield to hook a rooftop ahead. Hold to swing, then release to fly with your momentum. Dragging does not add speed. Use the buttons at the top to pause or change sound.

Levels and shields

The campaign has 60 progressively more challenging levels. Completing a level shows its name while the run continues. Each stage starts with four base shields. Upgrades can change your abilities and protection.

Gold and upgrades

Collect gold while playing and spend it in the Shop on character looks and upgrades, including Rooftop Jump and Glide Sail. All Shop items use earned gold. There are no real-money in-app purchases.

Progress and troubleshooting

Progress is stored on your device. If sound is off, check the game’s sound button and device volume. If performance drops, try Low Graphics in Settings and close other demanding apps. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot: deleting it can erase your local progress. Contact support before taking that step.

App Store purchases

Apple manages download billing and refund requests through Report a Problem. The developer cannot access your payment details.
